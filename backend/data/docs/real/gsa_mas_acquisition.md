Ordering procedures for MAS buying
Federal Supply Schedule ordering procedures
Procedures for placing orders and establishing blanket purchase agreements against MAS contracts can be found inGSAR subpart 538.71 (GSA Class Deviation RFO-2025-FSS-GSAR 538). The ordering procedures include guidelines on when you can place orders and applicable clauses. Find out more aboutusing BPAs for MAS products or services.
When you can place orders
You must place orders during the MAS contract’s period of performance. If you place an order during the POP but it’s not completed, the contractor must complete it by the time specified in the order according toFAR 52.216-22.
You can exercise options on orders beyond the ordering period of the MAS contract, including MAS contract option year periods. Look for fill-in language that provides for orders to extend up to 60 months beyond contract expiration according toFAR 52.216-22.
Find clauses applicable to an order
UseGSA eLibraryto view all clauses for that contract. On the contractor-specific information page, selectView the specifics for this contractlink underContract Clauses/Exceptions.
As much as possible, rely on MAS negotiated terms and conditions. You should review the terms and determine whether any further terms are necessary.
Order flexibilities
MAS ordering procedures include flexibilities such as order level materials, the OLM SIN, MAS contractor team arrangements, and the ability for MAS contractors to leverage other MAS contractors to supplement orders.Get details on these flexibilities.
